- Biomolecules are organic macromolecules synthesized by living organisms that dictate cellular structure, metabolism, and information flow. 
- They are built around carbon backbones due to carbon's ability to form four stable covalent bonds, often functionalized by oxygen, nitrogen, sulfur, and phosphorus.
