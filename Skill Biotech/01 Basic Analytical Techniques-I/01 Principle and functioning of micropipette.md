- A micropipette is laboratory equipment that is used to measure and transfer small volumes of samples, normally in the microliter range.
- In the market, they come in different sizes and ranges, but their principle is the same.

# Types of Micropipette:

1. **On the basis of volume**
     1. **Fixed volume micropipette:**
         In a fixed volume micropipette, there is a fixed amount of liquid to be aspirated or dispensed. It is used when the task of dispensing the same volume of liquid multiple times is involved.
     2. **Variable volume micropipette:**
         In a variable volume micropipette, the amount of liquid to be aspirated or dispensed can be changed according to the need. It has a specific limit and range of volume that can be aspirated/ dispensed.
 2. **On the basis of the principle of operation**
     1. **Air Displacement Micropipette:**
         It uses air displacement to draw a specific volume of liquid into the pipette’s barrel. The volume of liquid is determined by the calibrated volume markings on the barrel of the pipette.
     2. **Positive displacement Micropipette:**
         The piston in a positive displacement pipette makes direct contact with the sample, which allows the aspiration force to remain constant.
 3. **On the basis of the operating mechanism**
     1. **Mechanical Micropipette:**
         They are based on the spring piston principle and are operated using hand.
     2. **Electronic Micropipette:**
         They are automatic. The aspiration and dispense of the sample are done with a single touch button.
 4. **On the basis of the number of the channels**
     1. **Single-channel micropipette:**
        They have only one channel, which is used to aspirate and dispense the sample
     2. **Multi-channel micropipette:**
        They have multiple channels for aspirating and dispensing the sample. It is used to reduce the workload caused by a single-channel micropipette. They might have 12 channels, 8 channels or 16 channels.

# **Parts of Micropipette**
The basic parts of all micropipettes are:
1. **Plunger:** It is the topmost part of the micropipette. It is used to adjust the volume, aspirate and transfer the required volume of the sample.
2. **Tip ejector:** It is used to remove the micropipette tips easily by pressing the ejector.
3. **Volume window:** It shows the adjusted volume.
4. **Shaft:** It is a tube filled with air that is used to push or pull the liquid.
5. **Micropipette tips:** The tips are attached to the micropipette to collect and transfer samples.

# **Working mechanism of Micropipette**

When the plunger is pressed, the air inside the shaft forces the liquid in the micropipette tip to expel. Similarly, when the plunger is released, the piston moves upward, and the air is replaced by the liquid, so the liquid is collected in a micropipette tip.

# **Applications of Micropipette**

1. **Chemical Laboratories:** They are used to handle volatile and viscous samples
2. **Pharmaceutical Laboratories:** To assess quality control (QC) of pharmaceutical products, micropipettes are used in pharmaceutical laboratories
3. **Molecular biology Laboratories:** Different types of micropipettes are used in molecular biology labs to perform experiments related to PCR, western blot, etc.

---
# **Uses of Micropipettes**

1. They are used to prepare samples and reagents.
2. They are used to do slight mixing of samples by pressing and releasing plungers several times.
3. They are used to add a specific volume of solvent to the sample and dilute it.
4. They are used to transfer small volumes of samples from one tube to another.
5. They are used to measure and dispense small volumes of samples. This process is important during [ELISA](https://microbenotes.com/enzyme-linked-immunosorbent-assay-elisa/), [PCR](https://microbenotes.com/polymerase-chain-reaction-pcr-principle-steps-applications/), or other molecular biology experiments.
